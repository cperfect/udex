## Why does this exist?

This project started as a) a way to learn rust and b) to work through a solution I had in my head for a couple of problems I kept seeing in large integration projects. I think the first attempt dates back to 2021 and petered out pretty quickly. That got dumped and replaced with a fresh start in 2025. The big difference was AI - I could get claude to do the boring bits. Now learning how to use AI for development in a structured, reviewable, repeatable way has become a goal as well.
